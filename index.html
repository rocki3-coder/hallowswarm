Hallowswarm

What it is

A survival roguelite where you don't aim and you don't stop.

You're a trick-or-treater who took a wrong turn into a town where the holiday went wrong. Your charms fire on their own. Your only job is to move, choose well, and last the night.

Every run lasts about twenty minutes and ends one of two ways.

Three things that make it ours

Conjurings

Pair the right charm with the right hex and they fuse into something with its own name and its own rules. Ten to find. The grimoire fills in as you do.

Five haunted grounds

A rotting pumpkin patch, a cornfield maze, a moonlit graveyard, a haunted square, a witch's hollow. Each one bends the rules against you.

Your costume

Skeleton, witch, scarecrow, ghost, werewolf, vampire — built how you like, and your starting charm comes with it.
