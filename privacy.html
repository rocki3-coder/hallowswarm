01

Who we are

Hallowswarm is developed and published by DC Silent Studio Games (“we,” “us,” “our”). This policy explains what information the game collects when you install and play it, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

By installing or using Hallowswarm, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the game.

02

Information we collect

Information you give us directly

Hallowswarm does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to play. The only information you actively provide is what you send us voluntarily — for example, if you email our support address about a bug or a refund, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message.

Information collected automatically

When you play, the game and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect:

  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system version, screen resolution, language, region, and crash or performance diagnostics.
  • Advertising identifiers — your Android Advertising ID, and an app-set ID used to distinguish installs.
  • Gameplay and app activity — sessions started, runs completed, levels reached, and which in-game features you use. This is used to balance difficulty and fix problems.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address by our advertising partner, at country or region level. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Information stored only on your device

Your saved progress, unlocked costumes, upgrade tree, settings, and purchase entitlements are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your save file to our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes this local data; purchases made through Google Play can be restored on reinstall.

03

Advertising

Hallowswarm shows advertisements through Google AdMob. These include optional rewarded video ads you can choose to watch, and occasional ads between runs.

AdMob may use your advertising identifier and device data to select and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Google’s handling of that data is governed by its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Turning ads off

Hallowswarm offers a monthly ad-free subscription and a one-time Ad-Free Forever purchase. With either active, the game does not request or display ads and does not initialize the advertising SDK.

Limiting ad personalization

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads. In regions where consent is required, the game will show a consent prompt before any personalized advertising, and you can change your choice later from the in-game settings screen.

04

Purchases and subscriptions

All in-app purchases — candy corn bundles, costume packs, charm unlocks, and ad removal — are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card number, billing address, or any other payment credential. Google provides us only with a purchase token confirming that a transaction completed, which we use to unlock the content you bought and to restore it if you reinstall.

Subscriptions renew automatically. The monthly ad-free subscription continues until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play Store under Subscriptions; cancelling stops future charges and access continues until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Google under the Google Play refund policy.

05

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Run the game and save your progress and unlocked content
  • Deliver purchases and keep ad-free entitlements active across reinstalls
  • Show and measure advertising, unless you have removed ads
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve balance and performance
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and attempts to tamper with purchases
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles ourselves.

06

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers needed to operate the game:

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery and measurement
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing and purchase verification
  • Google Play Services — app distribution, crash reporting, and device integrity checks

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid government request, or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the game. If our studio is ever acquired or merged, information may transfer as part of that transaction; we will update this policy if that happens.

07

How long we keep it

Progress and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the game. Purchase records are retained by Google for as long as its policies require, and we retain purchase tokens for as long as needed to honor your entitlements and meet tax and accounting obligations. Crash and diagnostic data is retained on a rolling basis, generally no longer than 90 days. Support emails are kept for up to two years.

08

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have these rights under the GDPR; residents of California have comparable rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Because the game does not use accounts, the fastest way to delete everything we hold on your device is to uninstall Hallowswarm. For anything held by our providers, email us at dcsilentstudiogames@gmail.com and we will action your request within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

09

Children

Hallowswarm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game is not enrolled in the Google Play Families program. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Where required, the game requests age-appropriate treatment signals from Google Play and disables personalized advertising for users identified as children.

10

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted connections for all network traffic and server-side verification of purchase receipts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11

International transfers

Our service providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

12

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. For material changes, we will give notice inside the game before the change takes effect. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
